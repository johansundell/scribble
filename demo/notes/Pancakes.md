# Pancakes

For 4. #home

- 3 dl flour
- 6 dl milk
- 3 eggs
- a pinch of salt
- butter for the pan

Whisk the flour, salt and half the milk smooth, then the rest of the milk and the eggs. Let it rest for 10 minutes. Fry thin in butter.
